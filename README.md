
# 📊 Project Analysis: Political Art Nights (2025/2026) – Association CAMBIO

## 📝 Executive Summary
This analysis evaluates participant satisfaction, acquisition channels, and audience retention across the 2025/2026 season of **Political Art Nights**. By cross-referencing turnout, feedback volume, relevance scores, and venue impact, this project provides data-driven recommendations to optimize marketing strategies and event planning for next year.

---

## 📈 Key Findings & Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### 1. Attendance & Feedback Volume (Reach vs. Response Rate)
* **Turnout Highlights:** *"There will be soup"* registered the highest attendance of the season with **42 participants**, followed by *"Do it yourself"* (36) and *"Who made your clothes"* (32). High attendance indicates successful promotion or strong thematic appeal.
* **Venue Impact:** Popular venues like **Kukulida** (host of the launch event *"Cultivating hopeful narratives"*) played a key role in driving early engagement and boosting visibility at the start of the season.

### 2. Event Relevance & Perceived Value (Top-Box 5/5 Rating)
* **Top Performers:** The events rated highest for relevance were *"Circles of Care"* (**90.0%** top-box rating), *"Sustainable Bike Tour"* (**83.3%**), and *"Who Made Your Clothes"* (**81.2%**).
* **The Attendance-Satisfaction Paradox:** While *"There will be soup"* attracted the largest crowd, it scored the lowest top-box relevance (**28.6%**). However, this lower score correlates with a low survey response rate (~30.9%), meaning feedback from large-capacity events may be skewed toward non-respondents or first-time attendees.

### 3. Discovery Channels: Word-of-Mouth Dominance
* **Channel Breakdown:** An overwhelming **70% of respondents** discovered the events through a friend (*word-of-mouth*), while digital channels like Telegram and Instagram generated minimal reach.
* **Strategic Insight:** While word-of-mouth reflects a highly engaged community, it limits audience diversification. The association is primarily reaching "friends of friends," highlighting an urgent need to revamp social media outreach and poster placement to attract broader demographics.

### 4. Audience Loyalty & Retention
* **Overall Loyalty:** Across all events, the baseline intent to return is strong, with an **overall top-box fidelity rate of 67.0%**.
* **First-Timer Effect:** The fidelity score drops sharply for *"There will be soup"* (**38.5%**). Because this event drew a large volume of new attendees, lower initial retention is expected compared to niche events hosted for a core group of recurring participants.

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations for Next Season

1. 🎯 **Diversify Acquisition:** Shift marketing effort toward Instagram and local posters to break out of the "word-of-mouth bubble."
2. 📱 **Improve In-Person Feedback Collection:** Implement quick QR-code feedback moments during high-turnout events (like *"There will be soup"*) to improve survey response rates.
3. 📍 **Capitalize on Key Venues:** Partner continuously with highly rated venues (such as *Outside* or *Kukulida*) that consistently yield high average satisfaction scores (>4.7/5).
